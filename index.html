SalesTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SalesTrack handles information when you use the SalesTrack application for sales, products, inventory, orders, and customer management.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, SalesTrack may handle account information used for sign-in, such as your email address and authentication identifiers. Information entered into the app may include product names, prices, stock quantities, sales records, order details, and customer names or contact details.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate users, operate the app, manage sales and inventory, maintain product and order records, manage customer information, and display business summaries and reports.

3. Local Storage and Firebase

SalesTrack uses local database storage for app records. It also uses Firebase services provided by Google for authentication and cloud storage. Depending on the features used and the app's configuration, account information and business records may be transmitted to and stored by these services.

Google processes information as part of providing its services. For information about Google's data practices, see Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Sharing of Information

SalesTrack does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google Firebase to provide authentication and cloud storage. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect legal rights and security.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may remain in local storage or cloud storage while needed to provide the app's features. Deleting the app from a device may not delete information stored in Firebase.

To request deletion of your account and associated personal information, contact the privacy email below. Requests will be verified and processed, and associated account data will be deleted where applicable, subject to any legal obligations to retain specific information.

If account creation is available, SalesTrack must also provide an accessible in-app account deletion option. This option should delete the account and associated data, not merely disable the account.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by SalesTrack. Firebase and other services have their own security measures. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

SalesTrack is a business-management application and is not specifically designed for children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

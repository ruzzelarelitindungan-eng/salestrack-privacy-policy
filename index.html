SalesTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SalesTrack handles information when you use the SalesTrack application for sales, inventory, products, orders, and customer management.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, information entered into SalesTrack may include product names, prices, stock quantities, sales records, order details, and customer names or contact information. Please enter only information needed for your business activities.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the app's business-management features, maintain sales and inventory records, manage orders and customers, and display relevant summaries and reports.

3. Storage and Third-Party Services

SalesTrack uses local database technology to store app records on the device. Certain versions or features may also use Firebase services provided by Google for authentication or cloud data storage. Where these services are enabled, relevant information may be processed by those services to provide the feature. Their handling of information is also subject to Google's applicable privacy terms.

Do not assume that records are backed up or synchronized to the cloud unless that feature is available and enabled in your version of the app.

4. Sharing of Information

SalesTrack does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate enabled app features, or disclosed when required by law. The person using the app is responsible for having permission to enter and manage customer information.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Records may remain stored on your device until you delete them through the app, if that option is available, or remove the app and its local data. Data stored by enabled cloud services may require separate deletion. Uninstalling the app may not delete cloud-stored information.

6. Security

Reasonable care should be taken to protect the device and any account used with SalesTrack. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

SalesTrack is a business-management application and is not designed specifically for children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
